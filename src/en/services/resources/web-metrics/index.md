<!-- NOTE: these dimensions are aligned with the Universal Cake Evaluation Metrics: inclusive design (accessibility), environment (sustainability), security (privacy and security), agency and longevity, plus performance and visibility. -->

A good website works for everyone who visits it, loads quickly, can be found, uses little energy, protects its visitors, respects their choices and lasts. Each of these qualities can be checked, and most of the tools are free.
