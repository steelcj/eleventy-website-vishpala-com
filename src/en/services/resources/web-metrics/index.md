These qualities are directly linked to our company's values. In a nutshell, we strive to create technology that supports the wellbeing of life on our planet and that better serves its people. We consider these values foundational to building technology that enables this.

It all starts with communicating with our audiences in ways that are accessible to them. Many treat accessibility as a feature, or a nice extra. For us, accessibility means including everyone as equal partners, because that is what it takes to build great relationships and effective communication.

Our platforms and services are designed from the ground up to be usable by as wide an audience as possible. That includes people who use other languages, different devices, operating systems and assistive technologies, and people of all ages. It also includes people with older computers, or on slow, expensive or intermittent internet connections. A lightweight site or application that loads quickly and works well on modest hardware reaches more people and works better for everyone. And none of it helps if people can't find it, so we build sites that search engines and other tools can read and understand.

Wellbeing also requires respect. We protect the privacy and security of our clients and the people who visit and use the sites and applications we build, and we avoid design tricks that push them into choices they didn't mean to make. Respect extends to the planet too: we aim to use no more energy or resources than an excellent site or application really needs.

Finally, we want the people and organizations who use our tools to stay in full control of their own work. That is why content created with our tools is kept in open, portable formats, such as Markdown and standard image and document files, so that it can be moved, reused or taken elsewhere at any time. We build sites and applications to last, and to remain under the control of their owners and the people they serve.

Each of the qualities below reflects one part of that commitment, and each comes with free tools you can use to check any website, including ours.
