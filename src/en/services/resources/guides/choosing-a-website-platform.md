Choosing a website platform is often treated as a design decision. It is also a decision about who controls your content, what it will cost to change your mind, and who will be able to use your site. These questions help bring those trade-offs into the open before you commit.

## Ownership and leaving

- **Where is my content stored, and in what format?** Look for plain, open formats you can read and back up yourself, rather than a proprietary database only the vendor can open.
- **Can I export everything, including translations, images and page structure?** Ask for a sample export before you sign up, and check whether it is complete.
- **What would it take to move to another platform in three years?** If the honest answer is "rebuild the site", the platform owns more of your work than you do.
- **What happens if the vendor raises prices, changes its terms or is sold?** Read the terms of service with this question in mind.

## Accessibility

- **Does the platform produce pages that meet the WCAG guidelines by default?** Ask whether this has been tested with screen readers and keyboard-only navigation, not just claimed.
- **Can editors add alternative text, captions and transcripts easily?** Accessibility that depends on extra effort from busy staff tends not to happen.
- **Can you offer alternative formats, such as audio or easy-read versions, linked to the original page?**

## Languages

- **Is each translation linked to its original?** The language switcher should take visitors to the same page in the other language, not back to the home page.
- **Can you see which translations are out of date?**
- **Can different people edit different languages?**

## Bandwidth and devices

- **How large is a typical page?** Ask for real numbers, and test a few pages yourself on a slow connection.
- **Does the site work on older phones and browsers?**
- **Can critical information be used offline?**

## Where your data lives

- **In which country are your content and your visitors' data stored?**
- **Can you choose your hosting provider, or host the site yourself?**
- **Who can access your content and your visitors' data, and under which laws?**
