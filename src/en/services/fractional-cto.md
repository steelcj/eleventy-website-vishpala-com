## Who it's for

Nonprofits, NGOs and small organizations that make important technology decisions but cannot justify, or find, a full-time Chief Technology Officer.

## The problem

Small organizations often make technology decisions under pressure, guided by whoever is selling. The result can be tools that do not fit, costs that keep rising, data held by vendors, and systems no one on staff fully understands.

## What we do

Christopher Steel acts as your part-time technology lead. He brings three decades of experience in systems administration, automation, research computing and accessible web development, including serving as a co-founder and CTO. The focus is on decisions that protect your mission: fewer dependencies, more control, and technology your team can actually use.

## What you get

- A technology strategy aligned with your mission and budget
- Independent advice on software, platforms and vendors
- Planning for accessibility, multilingual communication and data sovereignty
- Oversight of infrastructure, backups and security basics
- Clear documentation, so your knowledge stays with your organization

## How it works

1. **Assess** your current technology, costs and risks.
2. **Plan** priorities for the next one to three years.
3. **Lead** on an agreed number of hours or days per month.
4. **Hand over** knowledge, so you are never dependent on us either.
