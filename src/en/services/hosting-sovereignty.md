## Who it's for

Organizations that want to know exactly where their content and data are stored, prefer Canadian providers, or need to keep control of their infrastructure for policy, privacy or resilience reasons.

## The problem

When your website platform, your content and your hosting all belong to the same vendor, you have little say over where your data lives or what happens if that vendor changes its prices, its terms or its ownership.

## What we do

We separate the three. Your content archive can be stored wherever you choose, with backups. Your website can be hosted with a Canadian provider, on your own server, or both. Because every layer is open source and portable, any of them can be moved later.

## What you get

- Your content archive stored in Canada or on your own systems
- Website hosting with the provider of your choice, or managed by Vishpala
- Regular, tested backups you can access yourself
- Documentation of where everything lives and how to move it
- Infrastructure planned to be migrated over time, not locked in

## How it works

1. **Decide** where your content and website should live.
2. **Set up** hosting, backups and monitoring.
3. **Document** the setup so you, or anyone you choose, can manage it.
4. **Maintain**, if you want ongoing support.
