## Who it's for

Organizations that want to move away from their current website platform, whether because of cost, complexity, security, foreign hosting or a desire for more control.

## The problem

Moving a website usually means rebuilding it. Content is exported in messy formats, links break, translations lose their connections, and the same problem waits for you at the next migration.

## What we do

We move your content into a Vishpala content archive first, then publish it to your new platform through a publishing connector. Your content is cleaned up, structured and linked once. After that, changing platforms again is a matter of pointing the archive somewhere new.

## What you get

- Your existing pages, posts, media and translations moved into an archive you own
- Translations linked to their originals, ready for language and medium toggles
- Redirects from old addresses so search rankings and bookmarks keep working
- A new site on your chosen platform, often lighter, faster and more accessible than the old one
- No dependency on any single vendor for the future

## Common migrations

- From hosted WordPress or plugin-heavy WordPress sites to Eleventy
- From proprietary website builders to open source platforms
- Toward platforms and hosting based in Canada

[Moving off WordPress](/en/solutions/move-off-wordpress/) · [Keeping your content in Canada](/en/solutions/canadian-content-hosting/)

## How it works

1. **Audit** your current site, content and traffic.
2. **Import** content into your archive and link translations.
3. **Publish** to the new platform and set up redirects.
4. **Switch over** once you have reviewed the new site.
