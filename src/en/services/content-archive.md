## Who it's for

Organizations that publish in more than one language, or want to, and anyone who wants their content to outlive their current website.

## The problem

On most websites, content is trapped inside the platform. Translations are separate pages that drift out of sync. Metadata is scattered across plugins. When it is time to redesign or change platforms, everything has to be copied, cleaned up and rebuilt by hand.

## What we do

We set up a source archive that holds your content independently of any website. Each piece of content is recorded as a work with a permanent identifier, and each translation or alternative format is linked to it as an expression. [Read about the model](/en/approach/one-work-many-expressions/).

## What you get

- A structured archive of your content in open, plain-text formats you can read and back up yourself
- Permanent identifiers linking every translation and format to its original
- Rich, library-grade metadata stored once and reused everywhere
- Editors assigned by language or format, each working on their own versions
- Clear visibility into which translations need updating when an original changes

## How it works

1. **Review** your existing content, languages and audiences.
2. **Design** the archive structure and metadata for your needs.
3. **Import** existing content and link translations to their originals.
4. **Train** your editors on creating, editing and pushing changes.
