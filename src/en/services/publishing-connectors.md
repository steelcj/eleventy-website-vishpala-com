## Who it's for

Anyone with a Vishpala content archive who wants changes to appear on their website without copying and pasting, and anyone who wants the option to change website platforms later.

## The problem

When a website platform is also where your content lives, the two can never be separated. Your choice of platform becomes permanent, and every redesign or migration puts your content at risk.

## What we do

A publishing connector reads your content archive and publishes it to a specific website platform. Internally we call these egressors, because they carry content out of the archive. An editor pushes their changes, and the connector takes care of the rest: pages, translations, alternative formats and metadata all arrive on your site together.

## What you get

- Automated publishing whenever editors push changes
- Translations and alternative formats published together, with language and medium toggles linked correctly
- Search and social metadata generated from your archive's richer metadata, without loss
- The freedom to add a second connector and publish the same archive to a different platform

## Supported platforms

- **Eleventy:** supported today, including multiple Eleventy versions
- **Drupal:** in development
- **Others:** connectors can be built for most CMSs and static site generators

## How it works

1. **Choose** your website platform, or keep the one you have.
2. **Configure** the connector for your site's structure and languages.
3. **Push** changes from the archive, and they appear on your site.
