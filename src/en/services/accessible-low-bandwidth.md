## Who it's for

Organizations whose audiences include older adults, people with disabilities, rural and remote communities, people using older phones and computers, or anyone on a slow, expensive or unreliable connection.

## The problem

Many modern websites are heavy. They load large scripts and images, assume fast internet and new devices, and treat accessibility as an afterthought. The people most likely to be left out are often the people an organization most wants to reach.

## What we do

We build static websites that are light by design. Pages load quickly, use little bandwidth and processing power, and follow accessibility standards from the start. Because your content lives in an archive with linked expressions, you can add alternative formats such as audio, easy-read or Braille-ready versions over time.

## What you get

- Pages built to WCAG accessibility guidelines and tested with screen readers
- Small page sizes that load well on slow and intermittent connections
- Sites that run well on older devices and browsers
- A medium toggle, alongside the language toggle, for alternative formats as you add them
- A lower energy footprint than typical dynamic websites
