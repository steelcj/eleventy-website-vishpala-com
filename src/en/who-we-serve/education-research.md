## What these groups need

- Project and lab websites that survive staff turnover and funding cycles
- Publications, datasets and learning materials described with good metadata
- Materials in several languages and accessible formats
- Infrastructure that fits institutional policies on data and hosting

## How Vishpala helps

**Built on library principles.** Vishpala's archive uses the same work and expression model libraries use to catalogue materials. [How it works](/en/approach/one-work-many-expressions/)

**Rich metadata, reused everywhere.** Describe content once, in detail, and publish it in the formats search engines and social platforms expect.

**Accessible learning materials.** Link easy-read, scientific, audio and other versions to each original. [Alternative formats](/en/solutions/alternative-formats/)

**Research computing experience.** Christopher Steel ran research systems infrastructure for neuroinformatics and health research at McGill, and understands institutional environments.
