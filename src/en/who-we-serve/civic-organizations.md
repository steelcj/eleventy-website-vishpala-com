## What these organizations need

- Information published quickly, in several languages at once
- Content that reaches every voter, member or community member, including people with disabilities and people on poor connections
- Many contributors, with clear control over who can edit what
- Confidence about where content and data are stored

## How Vishpala helps

**Editors by language and team.** Assign editors to each language or section. They push changes, and a [publishing connector](/en/services/publishing-connectors/) updates the site.

**Every format, linked.** Plain-language summaries, audio and other [alternative formats](/en/solutions/alternative-formats/) are linked to the original, so everyone gets the same information.

**Works on any platform.** Publish to Eleventy today, with Drupal and other platforms in development, from the same archive.

**Stored where you decide.** [Canadian or self-hosted storage](/en/services/hosting-sovereignty/) for your content archive and website.
