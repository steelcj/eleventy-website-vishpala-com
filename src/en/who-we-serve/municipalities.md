## What small municipalities face

- Small teams responsible for many sites and systems
- Storms and outages that cut power and internet exactly when people need information
- Residents who need services in more than one language, and in accessible formats
- Pressure to keep public data in Canada

## How Vishpala helps

**Resilient infrastructure.** Planning and setup for IT that keeps working across several sites through storms and outages.

**Information that works offline.** Emergency procedures and other critical information published as a lightweight static site that staff and residents can download and use with no connection.

**Bilingual and accessible.** Public information in every official language, linked and kept in step, in formats residents can use. [Accessible websites](/en/services/accessible-low-bandwidth/)

**Data kept in Canada.** [Canadian or municipal hosting](/en/services/hosting-sovereignty/) for content and backups.
