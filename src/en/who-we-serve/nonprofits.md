## What nonprofits tell us

- Our audiences speak different languages, and keeping translations current is hard.
- Many of the people we serve are older, use older devices or have poor internet.
- We are paying more every year for a platform we don't control.
- We don't have anyone on staff who can make confident technology decisions.

## How Vishpala helps

**Every audience, one archive.** A [multilingual content archive](/en/services/content-archive/) keeps every translation linked to its original, so nothing falls out of date unnoticed.

**Accessible by default.** [Light, accessible websites](/en/services/accessible-low-bandwidth/) work for older adults, assistive technology and slow connections, and can grow to include audio, easy-read and other formats.

**Costs that stay predictable.** Open source tools and static sites keep hosting costs low, and [your content stays yours](/en/services/hosting-sovereignty/) whichever provider you choose.

**Leadership without a full-time hire.** A [fractional CTO](/en/services/fractional-cto/) gives you senior, independent technology guidance a few days a month.
