## An idea borrowed from librarians

Libraries have long faced the problem of keeping track of many versions of the same thing: a novel, its translations, its audiobook, its large-print edition. Library cataloguing models, known as FRBR and now the IFLA Library Reference Model, solve it by separating the **work** (the idea itself) from its **expressions** (each specific realization of it, such as a translation or an audio recording).

Vishpala applies the same model to your website content.

## How it works

1. **Every piece of content is a work with a permanent identifier.** That identifier stays the same no matter how many versions exist.
2. **Each version is linked to the work as an expression.** A French translation, an audio recording, a Braille-ready file, a plain-language summary and a sign language video can all be expressions of the same work.
3. **Visitors choose what suits them.** Alongside the familiar language toggle, your site can offer a **medium toggle**. Someone using a Braille display, or who prefers audio or easy-read, picks that once and gets it across the site wherever it exists.

## What this makes possible

- **Accessibility that grows over time.** You can launch with text in two languages and add audio, easy-read or other formats later, without restructuring your site.
- **Translations that never drift out of sync.** Because every version points to the same work, it is easy to see which translations need updating when the original changes.
- **Better search results.** Rich metadata stored once in your archive is converted, without loss, into the formats search engines and social platforms use.
- **Freedom to change platforms.** The relationships between works and expressions live in your archive, not in a website plugin, so they move with you.

## From archive to website

You create and edit content in your source archive. Editors can be assigned by language or format, so your English editor and your French editor each work on their own expressions. When an editor pushes their changes, a publishing connector carries them to your website.
