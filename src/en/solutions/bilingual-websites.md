In Québec and across Canada, many organizations need to publish in both official languages, and often in others too. Keeping translations accurate and in step is where most websites struggle.

## The usual problems

- Translations that quietly fall out of date when the original changes
- A language switcher that drops visitors on the wrong page or the home page
- Translation plugins that break with updates
- No clear way to see what still needs translating

## How Vishpala does it

Every page is a work with a permanent identifier, and each translation is an expression of that work. [How it works](/en/approach/one-work-many-expressions/)

- **The language toggle always lands on the same page** in the other language.
- **Out-of-date translations are easy to spot,** because each one is tied to the version of the original it was made from.
- **Editors work by language.** Your French editor edits French, your English editor edits English, and each pushes their own changes.
- **Clean, language-specific addresses** such as /en/ and /fr/, which search engines handle well.

Adding a third or fourth language later works the same way.
