A translation is not the only way to express the same content. Some people need it read aloud, some need it in plain language, and some use a Braille display. Most websites have no good way to offer these.

## A medium toggle, beside the language toggle

Vishpala links each alternative format to its original as another expression of the same work. On your site, visitors get a **medium toggle** next to the language toggle. Someone who chooses audio, easy-read or Braille-ready text gets that format wherever it exists.

## Formats you can add

- Audio recordings or narrated versions
- Easy-read and plain-language versions
- Braille-ready text
- Sign language video
- Scientific or technical versions for specialist readers

You don't need all of these at once. Start with what your audience needs most, and add others over time without restructuring your site.
