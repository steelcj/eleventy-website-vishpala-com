WordPress powers a large share of the web, and the software itself is open source. Many organizations still find that their site has grown dependent on a particular host, a stack of plugins, or a theme vendor, and that it has become slow, costly to maintain, or hard to secure.

## Why organizations leave

- Plugin updates that break the site, or security patches that never stop
- Hosting and subscription costs that keep rising
- Translation plugins that are fragile or expensive
- A wish to move hosting to Canada or to their own systems

## How Vishpala does it

1. **Export and clean.** We bring your pages, posts, media and translations out of WordPress.
2. **Archive.** Your content goes into a [source archive you own](/en/services/content-archive/), with translations linked to their originals.
3. **Publish.** A [publishing connector](/en/services/publishing-connectors/) builds your new site, on Eleventy or another platform.
4. **Redirect.** Old addresses point to new ones, so search rankings and bookmarks keep working.

## The difference afterwards

Your next migration, whenever it comes, starts from a clean archive instead of a tangle of plugins. That is the point: you never have to do this kind of rescue again.
