More Canadian organizations are choosing Canadian providers, and want to know where their content and data actually live. With most website platforms, that is hard to guarantee and harder to change.

## How Vishpala does it

**Your archive, your location.** Your content archive is stored in Canada, on your own systems, or both, with backups you can access.

**Your website, your choice of host.** Publish to a Canadian hosting provider, your own server, or anywhere else you choose.

**Change without loss.** Because the archive is separate from the website, you can switch hosting providers or platforms without rebuilding your content.

**Open source throughout.** No proprietary layer that only one company can run.
