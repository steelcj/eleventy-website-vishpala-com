Rural and remote communities, people on prepaid data, people using older phones, and anyone caught in a storm or outage all have the same problem: heavy websites don't load.

## How Vishpala does it

- **Static pages.** No database queries or heavy scripts on each visit, so pages arrive quickly.
- **Small by design.** Images, fonts and scripts are kept to what each page needs.
- **Works on older devices.** Pages are built to standard, accessible HTML that older browsers handle well.
- **Offline-ready where it matters.** Critical information, such as emergency procedures, can be packaged for download and used with no connection.
- **Low energy use.** Smaller pages and simpler servers use less electricity.
